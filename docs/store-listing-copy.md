## English

- App Name: Merge Camp
- Subtitle: A merge puzzler with rising natural pressure
- Short Description: Place tiles on a 4x4 board, chain merges, and survive longer as pressure steadily rises.
- Promotional Text: Build efficient merge chains, control board pressure, and push for a new peak tier.
- Keywords: merge, puzzle, strategy, board, offline, single-player, casual
- Full Description:

Merge Camp is a mobile merge puzzle built around calm early turns and steadily rising board pressure.

Place camp tiles on a 4x4 grid, connect groups of three or more matching tiles, and turn careful positioning into higher-tier merges. There is no visible countdown timer. Instead, every placement tightens the board and raises the tension naturally as your session unfolds.

Features:
- One-tap mobile controls
- Deterministic merge rules you can learn and master
- Natural pressure escalation without a hard session timer
- Audio and haptic feedback for placement, merges, and warnings
- Offline single-player gameplay
